Regular physical activity improves cardiovascular health, strengthens muscles and bones, and helps maintain a healthy body weight. Exercise also supports mental health by reducing stress and improving mood. Common forms of exercise include walking, running, swimming, cycling, and strength training. Healthcare organizations recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week for adults. Consistent exercise lowers the risk of heart disease, diabetes, hypertension, and obesity. Active lifestyles contribute significantly to disease prevention and overall well-being.
