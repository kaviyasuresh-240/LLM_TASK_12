Nutrition is the process by which the body obtains essential nutrients from food. A balanced diet provides carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water. Proper nutrition supports growth, energy production, immune function, and disease prevention. Poor dietary habits can contribute to obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and nutritional deficiencies. Healthcare experts encourage consuming fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats while limiting excessive sugar, salt, and processed foods. Good nutrition contributes to long-term health and improves overall quality of life.
