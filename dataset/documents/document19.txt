Wearable health technologies include smartwatches, fitness trackers, and medical monitoring devices that collect health-related information in real time. These devices can monitor heart rate, sleep quality, physical activity, blood oxygen levels, and other health indicators. Healthcare providers may use wearable data to monitor patient conditions remotely and support preventive healthcare efforts. Wearable technology promotes patient engagement and encourages healthy lifestyle choices. Advances in sensor technology continue to expand the role of wearable devices in healthcare.
