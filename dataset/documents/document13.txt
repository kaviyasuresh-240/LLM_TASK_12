Anxiety disorders involve excessive fear, nervousness, and worry that interfere with normal activities. Symptoms may include rapid heartbeat, restlessness, sweating, difficulty concentrating, and sleep disturbances. Anxiety can be triggered by stressful situations, trauma, genetics, or medical conditions. Treatment often involves cognitive behavioral therapy, medications, relaxation techniques, and lifestyle changes. Managing anxiety effectively helps improve mental and physical health. Healthcare providers emphasize the importance of stress reduction, regular exercise, balanced nutrition, and adequate sleep to support emotional stability and overall well-being.
