Depression is a common mental health disorder characterized by persistent sadness, loss of interest in activities, fatigue, and changes in sleep or appetite. It can affect people of all ages and may significantly impact daily functioning, relationships, and work performance. Causes of depression may include genetic factors, stressful life events, chronic illnesses, and chemical imbalances in the brain. Treatment options include psychological counseling, medication, lifestyle modifications, and support groups. Healthcare professionals recommend seeking help early because untreated depression can worsen over time. Effective management can help individuals regain emotional well-being and improve their quality of life.
