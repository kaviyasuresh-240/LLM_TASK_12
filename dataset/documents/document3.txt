Hypertension, also known as high blood pressure, is a condition in which the force of blood against artery walls remains consistently high. Risk factors include obesity, smoking, excessive salt intake, lack of exercise, and stress. Hypertension can increase the likelihood of heart disease, stroke, and kidney failure. Many people do not experience symptoms, which is why it is often called a silent condition. Managing hypertension includes regular physical activity, maintaining a healthy weight, consuming a balanced diet, and taking prescribed medications.
