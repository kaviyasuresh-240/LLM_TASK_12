Robotic surgery is an advanced medical technology that assists surgeons in performing complex procedures with enhanced precision and control. Using robotic systems, surgeons operate specialized instruments through small incisions while viewing magnified images of the surgical site. Benefits include reduced blood loss, smaller scars, shorter hospital stays, and faster recovery times. Robotic surgery is commonly used in cardiovascular procedures, urology, gynecology, and cancer treatments. Although robots assist with surgical tasks, medical professionals remain responsible for decision-making and patient care throughout the procedure.
