Sleep plays a critical role in maintaining physical and mental health. During sleep, the body repairs tissues, strengthens the immune system, and supports cognitive functions such as memory and learning. Adults generally require seven to nine hours of quality sleep each night. Poor sleep can increase the risk of obesity, diabetes, heart disease, depression, and reduced concentration. Healthy sleep habits include maintaining a consistent bedtime schedule, limiting screen exposure before sleep, and creating a comfortable sleeping environment. Healthcare professionals recognize sleep as an essential component of overall wellness.
