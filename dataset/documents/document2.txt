Diabetes is a chronic health condition that affects how the body processes blood sugar. Type 1 diabetes occurs when the body cannot produce insulin, while Type 2 diabetes occurs when the body does not use insulin effectively. Common symptoms include increased thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, and blurred vision. Managing diabetes often involves healthy eating, regular exercise, monitoring blood glucose levels, and taking medications when needed. Early diagnosis and treatment help prevent complications such as kidney disease, nerve damage, and heart problems.
