Mental health refers to the emotional, psychological, and social well-being of an individual. It influences how people think, feel, and behave in daily life. Good mental health helps individuals cope with stress, maintain healthy relationships, and make effective decisions. Mental health can be affected by genetics, environment, lifestyle, and physical health conditions. Common mental health challenges include anxiety disorders, depression, and stress-related conditions. Healthcare professionals encourage regular exercise, healthy eating habits, sufficient sleep, and social support as ways to maintain positive mental well-being. Early recognition of mental health issues allows individuals to seek timely support and treatment, improving overall quality of life.
