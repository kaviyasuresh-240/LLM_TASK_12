Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare by improving diagnosis, treatment planning, and patient care. AI systems can analyze large volumes of medical data faster than traditional methods. Hospitals use AI to detect diseases early, improve workflow efficiency, and support doctors in making informed decisions. Machine learning algorithms are commonly used in medical imaging, predictive analytics, and drug discovery. AI does not replace healthcare professionals but enhances their ability to provide accurate and timely treatment. As technology advances, AI continues to play an important role in modern healthcare systems.
